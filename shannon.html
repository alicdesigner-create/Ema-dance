TEACHER

SHANNON LEE

Hey, I'm Shannon. I've been moving my whole life. From the time I was young, hip-hop and commercial dance were part of my world — until life pulled me away for a few years. Then one adult heels class changed everything.

That class didn't just bring me back to dance — it opened the door to a whole new way of moving, one that invites you to take up space, own your presence, and embrace your femininity without apology. I've been training in heels ever since. After subbing classes, leading my own sessions, and hosting workshops, I'm finally ready to offer what I've been building toward: my first fully curated course, created from the ground up for you.

My teaching style is technical and disciplined because I care deeply about the craft. But what matters most to me is you — the person showing up in the room. No prior dance experience is required. All you need to bring is the willingness to show up and be seen. Heels isn't about perfection. It's about becoming.
